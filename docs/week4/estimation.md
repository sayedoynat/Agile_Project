# Estimation Approach

## Estimation Technique

Our team uses Planning Poker with the Fibonacci
sequence to estimate User Stories.

## Fibonacci Scale

- 1 Point: Very simple task.
- 2 Points: Simple task.
- 3 Points: Medium complexity.
- 5 Points: High complexity.
- 8 Points: Very high complexity.
- 13 Points: Extremely complex task.

## Estimation Factors

We consider the following factors:

1. Implementation complexity.
2. Technical uncertainty.
3. Testing requirements.
4. Dependencies on other stories.
5. Business logic complexity.

## Estimation Process

1. The Product Owner explains the User Story.
2. Team members discuss the requirements.
3. Each member selects an estimate.
4. Different estimates are discussed.
5. The team agrees on a final Story Point value.

## Final Estimation

The team estimates each User Story
and records its Story Points in Jira.
